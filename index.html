Privacy Policy

Evolution World — Android mobile game

Effective date: October 14, 2026

ASTRA Games ("we", "us", or "our") publishes Evolution World (the "Game", package name com.astragames.evolutionworld). This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the Game, including by the third-party services it uses.

In short: the Game has no account or sign-up, and we do not run our own servers that receive your data. We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. Your progress stays on your device. Optional rewarded ads are provided by Google AdMob, and in-app purchases are processed by Google Play.

1. Game progress stored on your device

The Game stores your progress and preferences in local storage on your device, inside the Game's built-in web view. This includes unlocked creatures and eras, in-game currencies, upgrades, achievements, statistics, settings, and whether you accepted this policy. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted if you use Reset in the Game settings, clear the Game's storage, or uninstall the Game.

2. Internet connection check

The Game needs an internet connection. While the Game is open, it periodically checks whether you are online by sending an empty request to a Google server (https://www.gstatic.com/generate_204). The request contains no game data. Like any internet request, it reveals your IP address and basic technical information to Google.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to show optional rewarded ads. An ad is shown only when you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward. To deliver, measure and protect advertising, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects information and shares it with Google, such as:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • interactions with the Game and with ads, such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic information, such as performance and crash data related to ads;
  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID where available and permitted.

Google uses this information for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention and, where you have allowed it, ad personalization. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Consent and advertising choices

The Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a Google consent form is shown when the Game starts, before the advertising SDK is initialized. You can accept or decline personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad settings.

On every device you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on the device).

5. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Optional purchases, such as the premium Evolution Pass, are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. We never receive your card number or other payment details. The Game receives only a confirmation of the purchase (the product and its status) from Google Play, so it can unlock the item on your device. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.
  • We do not create accounts or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or contacts.
  • We do not request access to location, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

The Game requests these Android permissions: internet access, network state, Google Play Billing, and the advertising ID permission used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

7. Sharing and retention

Information is shared only with Google, as described in sections 2–5. Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. We do not hold your information on servers of our own. Google retains information according to its own policies; see How Google retains data.

8. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and its target audience in Google Play is set to ages 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game and you have concerns, contact us at the address below.

9. Your rights and requests

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Because we do not store your data on our servers, you can delete all game data yourself with Reset in the settings or by uninstalling the Game. For data processed by Google, you can use Google's privacy controls. For any privacy question or request, contact us at the address below.

10. Security

Connections made by the Game and by Google's SDKs use encryption in transit (HTTPS). No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Updates to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when the Game, its service providers or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date. A link to this page is always available in the Game under Settings → Privacy Policy.
